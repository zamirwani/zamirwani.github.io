Research interests

Lens Antennas

Thin planar lenses that shape antenna wavefronts to improve gain and support multiple beams. The work explores metasurface and anisotropic designs for low-profile MIMO systems.

Reconfigurable Metasurfaces

Planar electromagnetic structures for wave control, including reconfigurable surfaces, absorbers, and polarization conversion for communication and sensing.

Beamforming and MIMO Antennas

Compact and high-gain antennas with multiple ports, switched beams, and steerable radiation patterns for microwave and millimetre-wave communication.

Microwave Imaging and Sensing Systems

Frequency-diverse antennas and computational imaging methods for dielectric contrast mapping and non-contact sensing.

RF and Microwave Circuits

Microwave components that combine filtering, radiation, reconfiguration, and energy harvesting for wireless and sensing applications.

Metamaterials and Anisotropic Media

Engineered media for phase correction, enhanced aperture efficiency, and the routing of electromagnetic waves.

Funded research

Development of Frequency-Diverse Microwave Imaging System for Dielectric Contrast Mapping

This project combines frequency-diverse antenna design with computational reconstruction to study dielectric contrast via microwave imaging.
