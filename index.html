01 / RESEARCH

Controlling waves.
Creating new possibilities.

Research spanning electromagnetic structures, antenna systems, and the information carried by scattered fields.

01

Frequency-diverse microwave imaging

Metasurface antennas that produce distinct radiation patterns across frequency, enabling computational reconstruction with fewer measurement channels. A current application is non-invasive assessment of internal defects in apples.

Computational imagingMetasurfaces
02

Lens & multibeam antennas

Planar lenses, gradient-index structures and anisotropic media for gain enhancement and multiple radiation beams. The aim is compact beam control for microwave and millimetre-wave systems.

GRIN lensesBeam steering
03

Reconfigurable surfaces & MIMO

Reconfigurable metasurfaces and antenna architectures for flexible radiation control, alongside compact MIMO antennas for wireless communication terminals.

RISMIMO antennas
04

Microwave sensors & battery-free IoT

Research directions in RF sensing, wireless energy harvesting and antennas for low-power sensor systems, including non-invasive quality assessment and agricultural applications.

RF energy harvestingWireless sensing
